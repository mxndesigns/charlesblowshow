# 03 — Platform Strategy

*Each platform has ONE job. We don't post the same thing everywhere — we play each channel to its strength, all funneling to the owned audience (email + membership).*

---

## Platform roles at a glance

| Platform | Primary job | Funnel stage | Owned? |
|----------|-------------|--------------|--------|
| **YouTube** | Depth + discovery (the "home" of the show) | Awareness → Engagement | Rented |
| **Instagram** | Reach + personality (top of funnel) | Awareness | Rented |
| **Substack** | The owned audience + revenue | Engagement → Revenue | **Owned** |
| **LinkedIn** | Credibility + the influentials | Awareness → Trust | Rented |
| **charlesblowshow.com** | The hub everything points to | All stages | **Owned** |

**Golden rule:** rented platforms create *demand*; owned platforms *capture and monetize* it. Every post has a path to email signup.

---

## 🔴 YouTube — the home base

**Job:** Host the long-form conversations; be the searchable, evergreen library; primary discovery via search + suggested.

**Content:**
- **Daily spine:** ☀️ *Lunch Break Live* (10 min, live) + 🌙 *The Evening Show* (20 min, guest) — Mon–Fri. The live segment is great for live watch-time and the algorithm; the Evening Show builds the evergreen library.
- **Monthly tentpoles:** *Big Interview*, *State of the Unions*, *Roundtable*.
- **Shorts:** 6+ per day across both segments, hooks in first 2 seconds, captioned, vertical.
- **Live:** daily *Lunch Break Live*, monthly Town Hall, election-night & breaking-news specials.

**Optimization:**
- Thumbnails: high-contrast, Blow's face + guest + a tension-carrying phrase.
- Titles: curiosity + clarity (the question the episode answers).
- Chapters on every long-form video; pinned comment with the "What Now?" action + newsletter link.
- Playlists by segment franchise ("The Throughline," guest series).

**Cadence:** 2 segments/day (Mon–Fri), 6+ Shorts/day, monthly tentpoles + Town Hall live.

**KPIs:** watch-time hours, avg view duration / % completion, **subscriber growth toward the 10k monetization gate**, Shorts → long-form conversion, **clicks to newsletter from cards/description.**

## 📸 Instagram — reach & personality

**Job:** Widen the top of funnel; humanize Blow; convert scrollers into subscribers.

**Content:**
- **Reels:** *Blow's Brief*, best guest clips, "The Throughline" micro-explainers.
- **Carousels:** the week's argument in 6 slides; "Reading List"; quote sets.
- **Stories:** behind-the-scenes, polls/questions (community + research), episode countdowns, link stickers to site/Substack.
- **Broadcast Channel:** drop-link alerts to most-engaged fans.

**Optimization:** strong first-frame hook, baked-in captions, consistent visual template, trend-aware audio *only when on-brand*. Link in bio → site landing → email capture.

**Cadence:** 1 Reel/day (batched), 3–5 Stories/day, 2 carousels/week.

**KPIs:** reach, shares & saves (the real signals), follower growth, profile → link taps, link-tap → subscribe rate.

---

## ✍️ Substack — the owned audience & revenue engine

**Job:** This is the *product's balance sheet.* Convert attention into an email relationship and recurring revenue.

**Structure:**
- **Free tier:** weekly column + newsletter → reach & list growth.
- **Paid tier ($6–8/mo or ~$60/yr):** full archive, subscriber-only posts, ad-free podcast feed, monthly Town Hall access, comment community, Mailbag priority.
- **Founding member:** premium tier (events, annual call, recognition).

**Content:** the weekly Column, episode companion pieces, hot-take Notes, monthly long read, the weekly Newsletter digest. Use **Notes** as Substack's native social layer to recruit free subs.

**Optimization:** every episode and clip CTAs to subscribe; welcome sequence for new subs; periodic "free post that should be paid" to demonstrate value; paywall the back half of select deep pieces.

**Cadence:** 1 column + 1 newsletter/week, 2–4 Notes/week, 1 long read/month.

**KPIs:** **total subscribers (north star), paid conversion %, MRR/churn, open & click rates, reply rate.**

## 💼 LinkedIn — credibility & the influentials

**Job:** Reach operatives, journalists, academics, nonprofit/policy leaders — the people who *amplify and cite*. Also a B2B/sponsor & speaking-gig surface.

**Content:**
- Text-first essays (excerpts of the column reframed for a professional audience).
- Native-uploaded clips with a sharp written framing on top.
- "What this means for [civic life / institutions / democracy]" angle — substance over snark.
- Document/PDF carousels of the Reading List or key arguments.

**Optimization:** hook in first 2 lines (before "see more"), native video, post in professional-hours windows, engage in comments (LinkedIn rewards author replies). No engagement-bait.

**Cadence:** 3–4 posts/week.

**KPIs:** impressions among target titles, profile follows, comment quality, referral traffic to site/Substack, inbound (sponsor/speaking).

## Cross-platform operating model

**Single editorial calendar** governs everything. One theme per week flows across all channels in formats native to each.

```
            ┌──────────── THE CONVERSATION (weekly taping) ────────────┐
            │                                                          │
   YouTube ─┤ long-form + Shorts        Substack ─┤ column + newsletter│
            │                                      │  (CAPTURE EMAIL)  │
 Instagram ─┤ Reels + carousels + Stories          │                  │
            │                          LinkedIn ─┤ essays + clips      │
            └──────────► all CTAs ─────► charlesblowshow.com ◄─────────┘
                                          (email + membership)
```

**Adaptation, not duplication:** the same idea is a YouTube title, an IG hook, a LinkedIn thesis, and a Substack argument — phrased for each audience.

**Consistency system:** shared brand kit (logo, color, type, lower-thirds, templates) so the show is instantly recognizable in any feed.
