# 05 — Guest Roadmap: Incremental Conversations in the Political Space

*Booking is a product roadmap. You earn marquee guests by building an audience and a track record. Climb the ladder deliberately — each tier of guest unlocks the next.*

> **Note on names:** the people below are *illustrative archetypes and example targets* to show the booking strategy and trajectory. Treat them as a template, not a confirmed booking list — actual outreach depends on relationships, news cycles, and availability.

---

## The guest ladder (why incremental matters)

```
Tier 4  ▲  Marquee / national figures        ── earned by reach + reputation
Tier 3  │  Validators & rising stars         ── earned by a credible body of work
Tier 2  │  Practitioners & in-the-arena       ── earned by a few strong episodes
Tier 1  │  Reachable allies & experts         ── start here, build the reel
        └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────►
                        episodes / audience / credibility over time
```

You don't open by cold-pitching a sitting senator. You build 8–12 excellent episodes with accessible, high-substance guests, *then* the marquee names say yes because the show is now a real platform.

---

## Tier 1 — Foundation (Months 1–2): reachable, high-substance

Guests who are accessible, articulate, and aligned — to nail the format and build the highlight reel.

- **Academics & historians** — race, democracy, voting rights, political history (Blow's intellectual lane).
- **Authors with new books** — built-in promo motivation = easy yes; they bring their audience.
- **Local & state officials** — mayors, state legislators, DA/AG candidates; hungry for reach.
- **Movement & nonprofit leaders** — voting-rights orgs, civil-rights groups, policy shops.
- **Journalists & columnists** — Blow's peers; mutual-audience boost.

**Goal:** 8–12 episodes that prove the format, fill the clip library, and establish the brand.

---

## Tier 2 — Credibility (Months 3–4): in-the-arena practitioners

- **Sitting members of Congress** (House first — more accessible than Senate).
- **Strategists & operatives** — campaign managers, pollsters, comms directors.
- **Former officials** — ex-administration, ex-staffers who can speak freely.
- **Issue specialists** — economists, legal scholars (courts, civil rights), public-health voices.

**Goal:** establish the show as a serious stop on the political-media circuit.

---

## Tier 3 — Momentum (Months 5–8): validators & rising stars

- **Senators & governors.**
- **Rising national figures** — the next-cycle names before they're everywhere.
- **Major authors & public intellectuals** — the marquee thinkers.
- **Cultural figures with political weight** — artists, athletes, faith leaders who move opinion.

**Goal:** episodes that *make news* and get cited by other outlets.

---

## Tier 4 — Marquee (Months 9–12+): national figures

- **Presidential / national-ticket figures**, party leadership.
- **Globally recognized activists & icons.**
- **Headline newsmakers** — the "how did they get *that* interview?" bookings.

**Goal:** signature, talked-about interviews that define the brand and drive subscription spikes.

---

## Booking strategy & engine

| Lever | Tactic |
|-------|--------|
| **The book-tour pipeline** | Track upcoming political book releases; authors need press → reliable, high-quality guests. |
| **News-peg outreach** | When someone is central to a story, pitch *that week.* Speed wins bookings. |
| **The warm network** | Blow's existing relationships (NYT, MSNBC, author world) are tier-jumping shortcuts. |
| **Reciprocity** | Guests cross-promote to *their* audiences — bake sharing into the deal. |
| **Series & franchises** | "Governors series," "2026 midterms series" — packaging makes asks easier and bingeable. |
| **Make guests look good** | Reputation for fair, smart, well-distributed conversations becomes the best recruiting tool. |

---

## Balance & editorial integrity

- **Ideological range within principle:** primarily progressive-aligned, but include *"Devil's Advocate"* conversations with conservatives/dissenters where the exchange is honest and substantive — this builds credibility and shareability.
- **Demographic & geographic diversity:** not just D.C.; surface state/local and movement voices.
- **Avoid sameness:** vary archetype week to week (official → academic → organizer → author) so the grid stays fresh.

---

## Themed series roadmap (packaging the conversations)

| Series | Window | Concept |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **"The Stakes"** | Launch | Foundational conversations on democracy, race, power — sets the show's worldview. |
| **"Road to the Midterms"** | Election year ramp | Recurring check-ins with candidates, strategists, organizers. |
| **"The Throughline" specials** | Ongoing monthly | History-meets-now deep dives with scholars. |
| **"Next Up"** | Quarterly | Rising figures before they break nationally. |
| **"State of Us"** | Annual | Year-in-review marquee panel + special. |
