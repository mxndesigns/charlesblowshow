## The one line (use verbatim)

> **The show is free. Members fund independent journalism — and get the full archive, ad-free audio, and the members' room. The news itself is never paywalled.**

---

## The model: three legs

Independent political media survives on a **diversified** base, not one channel. CBS runs three:

1. **Memberships** (recurring, audience-funded — the durable core)
2. **Sponsorships** (host-read, brand-aligned — the near-term cash)
3. **Platform & ad revenue** (YouTube ads, podcast dynamic ads — scales with reach)

---

## Membership tiers (canonical)

| Tier | Price | Billing | What's included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | — | Every episode (both segments) + the free **weekly** dispatch. The news is never paywalled. |
| **Member** | **$7 / mo** or **$70 / yr** (save ~17%) | monthly/annual | Ad-free **early** audio, the **daily** dispatch, full searchable archive, members-only Q&A thread. |
| **Founding Member** | **$150 / yr** | annual | Everything in Member **+** name in the credits, quarterly members' live call, founding badge. **Limited launch cohort.** |

**Rules**
- ✅ Membership is **optional**; the free tier is fully functional.
- ✅ Annual is the nudge (two months free vs monthly).
- ❌ No tier gates the daily news segment itself.

## Platform & ad revenue

- **YouTube ads** on the free show (CPM varies by topic/season; model conservatively).

- Treated as upside, not the foundation — memberships + sponsorships carry the plan.

## Modeling assumptions (for the Revenue Model xlsx — editable)

Drive everything from an **Assumptions** sheet so changing one input recomputes the rest:

- YouTube subscribers ramp: Month-0 launch → **10,000** at the success-ladder milestone.
- View → email conversion; email → paid conversion (**model 2–5%**).
- Membership mix (share Member vs Founding); monthly vs annual split.
- Monthly churn (model **4–6%**).
- Sponsorship: # episodes/mo × avg reads × CPM × (audience/1000).
- Platform RPM for YouTube/podcast.
- Outputs: MRR, ARR, members, sponsorship revenue, blended revenue, 12-month projection.
