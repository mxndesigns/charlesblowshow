## 1. What it is (positioning)

**The Charles Blow Show** is a **daily, independent political-media show** — incisive analysis of race, politics, and power in America, in conversation. Two segments a day, a companion site, and a daily newsletter.

| Segment | Length | Cadence | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☀️ **Lunch Break Live** | ~10 min | Daily, midday, live | A tight, live read on the day's biggest story — reaction in real time, audience in the room. |
| 🌙 **The Evening Show** | ~20 min | Daily, evening | A deeper conversation with a high-profile guest — the people and ideas behind the headlines. |
| 📰 **The Daily Dispatch** | — | Daily, email | The column-grade take, distilled — what happened, why it matters, what to do with it. |

**Format ladder (Gelatin → CBS analog):** Gelatin recruited "founding DJs"; CBS recruits **founding members** and **launch guests**. Success ladder: **10,000 YouTube subscribers → audience growth → profitability** (from `docs/01`, `docs/06`).

## 3. Audience

Politically engaged adults who want to **understand** the day, not just feel angry about it: readers of opinion columns, NPR/PBS listeners, Substack subscribers, YouTube-news watchers. They arrive on a phone between things (lunch, commute, evening wind-down) and on desktop when they sit to read. Job-to-be-done: *"make sense of race, politics, and power today, from someone I trust, and leave me equipped instead of anxious."*

---

## 4. Brand personality & anti-references

**Incisive · Principled · Warm · Unflinching · Literate.** Voice: plainspoken and precise; the smart friend at the table, not a lecturer. Sentence case in UI; lowercase-with-period for editorial section titles ("the format.").

**Anti-references (what it must NOT look or sound like):**
- ❌ **Cable-news loud** — no red/white/blue chrome, breaking-news bombast, lower-third clutter.
- ❌ **Cold corporate / SaaS** — no sterile geometric-sans neutrality, startup-minimalism.
- ❌ **Partisan campaign** — no flag motifs, stars-and-stripes, slogan badges.
- ❌ **Loud podcast-bro** — no neon-on-black, mic icons, grunge, streamer aggression.

## 7. Monetization model — HYBRID (canonical, DRAFT numbers)

The show is **free** (YouTube, ad-supported + host-read sponsors). Revenue is **diversified across three legs**: memberships, sponsorships, and platform/ad revenue. *Numbers below are drafts to model, not committed prices — confirm before publishing.*

### Membership tiers
| Tier | Price (draft) | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | The full show + the free weekly dispatch. The news is never paywalled. |
| **Member** | **$7 / mo** or **$70 / yr** | Ad-free early audio, the full **daily** dispatch, complete archive, members-only Q&A thread. |
| **Founding Member** | **$150 / yr** | Everything in Member + name in the credits, a quarterly members' live call, founding badge. Limited launch cohort. |

### Sponsorship
- **Host-read sponsor slots** (pre-roll / mid-roll). Draft rate: **$25–$45 CPM**, or a flat **launch rate per episode** while audience builds.
- **Founding Sponsor** quarter package: a fixed number of reads + newsletter placement + a launch-partner mention. (Mirrors a flat, transparent fee — like Gelatin's "flat $7": **say the number, no surprises**.)

### The "one line" for each channel
- **Site / membership page:** *The show is free. Members get ad-free audio, the full archive, and the members' room — and keep independent journalism independent.*
- **Email:** *You can watch every day for free. Members fund the work and get the full dispatch, ad-free audio, and the archive.*
- **Verbal/pitch:** *"It's free to watch. If you want the full dispatch, the archive, and ad-free audio — and you want to keep this independent — membership is seven dollars a month."*

### Do / Don't
- ✅ Always say **the show is free** and **the news is never paywalled**.
- ✅ Call membership **optional**; the free tier is fully functional.
- ❌ Don't paywall the actual daily news segment.
- ❌ Don't quote sponsorship rates publicly until confirmed; keep them in the internal revenue model.
